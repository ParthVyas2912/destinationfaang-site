About Destination FAANG

Free education for everyone — because the best reward is lifting others up.

Hi, I'm Parth Vyas 👋

I created this channel and website because I am a true believer in free education. I want to help the people who have no other means of helping themselves, and give them a real chance to improve their lives.

Quality interview preparation shouldn't be locked behind expensive paywalls. Everything here — DSA, System Design, and Behavioral prep — is free, and it always will be. My goal is simple: to empower everyone to get better in their careers, no matter where they start.

“The greatest reward I can ever earn is seeing someone else rise.”
“Knowledge grows the moment you give it away. Free education isn't charity — it's how we lift each other up.”
“If even one person changes their life because of something I taught for free, every hour was worth it.”

A little about my background

I hold a Master's degree from Carleton University, and over my career I've had the privilege of working at companies like RBC, Nokia, Cineplex, and Microsoft. Along the way I learned exactly what it takes to clear tough technical interviews — and how overwhelming the journey can feel when you're doing it alone. Destination FAANG is my way of handing that map to everyone who comes after me.
